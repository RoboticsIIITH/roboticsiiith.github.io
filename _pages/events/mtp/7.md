### <a id="about-the-program"></a>About the Program

IIIT Hyderabad is a Resource Center (RC) for the MeitY-approved project entitled

*"Capacity Building for Human Resource Development in Unmanned Aircraft System (Drone and related Technology)"*.

As part of this initiative, the **Robotics Research Center (RRC), IIIT Hyderabad** is organizing the **Seventh Master Trainer Program (MTP)** under the Technology Specific Work Theme **"Allied UAS Technologies"**, with a focus on **Vision-based Navigation for UAS**, from **26th to 30th October 2026**.

The five-day program will cover theoretical concepts, practical aspects, and recent developments in vision-based navigation and autonomous UAV systems.


---

### <a id="program-topics"></a>Program Topics

#### Foundations of Computer Vision

Fundamentals of computer vision and their applications in autonomous UAV systems.

#### Motion Planning and Physical AI

Motion planning techniques and the role of Physical AI in autonomous navigation.

#### Topographic Mapping and Path Planning

Topographic mapping, environment representation, and path planning for UAV navigation.

#### Vision-Action Policies for Navigation

Vision-based action policies for autonomous navigation and decision-making.

#### Interpretable Robot Learning for Navigation

Learning-based approaches for navigation with emphasis on interpretability.

#### Integration of Classical Robotics with Modern AI

Integration of classical robotics methods with modern artificial intelligence techniques.

#### Applications of Vision-Language Models (VLMs)

Applications of Vision-Language Models for intelligent UAV perception and navigation.

#### Implementation of Autonomous Navigation

Practical implementation of autonomous navigation systems for UAVs.
