abstract: "This paper presents a centralized Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) agent-based framework for Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) based source-seeking for Uncrewed Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). The source is considered to be emitting a signal from an unknown location. The signal strength measured by individual UAVs drives the multi-UAV search in a Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) framework. We consider different variants of PSO like Acceleration-based PSO (APSO), Standard PSO (SPSO), and Adaptive Robotic PSO (ARPSO) that are commonly used in source-seeking problems. The coefficients of these PSO variants are selected by a PPO agent. The performance of the proposed method is demonstrated through extensive simulations highlighting time efficiency, sampling efficiency, and scalability with respect to the number of UAVs."
